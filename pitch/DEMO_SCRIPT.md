# ProofLine — 90-Second Judge Presentation & Demo Script

**Runtime:** ~1:30 · **Format:** Natural spoken paragraphs (one take)

---

## Spoken Script

Viral science headlines sound permanent, but scientific truth moves like a stock chart. One small study sparks a huge headline, and years later, bigger studies quietly show it wasn't even real. That’s why I built ProofLine—an interactive tracker and statistical lab where students can watch how scientific claims actually hold up over time.

Static PDFs hide the full story. You see the flashy original claim, not the bigger follow-up study that failed to repeat it. Textbook formulas don't stick, but watching the evidence line drop on a chart makes it click right away.

Take Power Posing. In 2010, a tiny study with 42 people claimed it boosts testosterone. Watch what happens as I drag the timeline forward: by 2015, a five-times-larger study finds almost zero effect, and by 2018, it flatlines right at zero. At every stop, ProofLine explains why the line moved and gives students a three-question checklist before they cite anything.

You can also stress-test the math live. Drag the Sample Size slider, trim out bias, and watch a shaky claim collapse across the zero line—then compare it to a verified control like smoking and lung cancer, which stays rock solid. Plus, one click turns on Hyperlegible mode for dyslexia-friendly spacing, or Audio Summary to hear the breakdown read out loud.

ProofLine runs 100% in the browser with React, TypeScript, and a custom statistical engine—ready for any classroom. Don't just read the headline—stress-test the proof.
